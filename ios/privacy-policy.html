Privacy Policy

Session Capture for iOS  ·  Effective August 5, 2026

The short version: Session Capture collects no data about you. Nothing you capture ever leaves your device unless you choose to export and share it yourself.

No data collection

Session Capture does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data. There are:

  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No user accounts
  • No servers operated by the developer

The developer never receives, sees, or has access to anything you do in the app.

Everything stays on your device

Transcripts, images, PDFs, and anything else you capture with the app are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere by the app.

The in-app browser

When you open a web page in the app's built-in browser — such as claude.ai, chatgpt.com, or gemini.google.com — your device communicates directly with that website, just as it would in Safari. Those connections are governed by each website's own privacy policy, not this one. The app itself transmits nothing to the developer.

Sign-in cookies

If you sign in to a website inside the in-app browser, any cookies are stored in Apple's system WebKit store on your device. The developer never reads or transmits them.

On-device AI features

The optional Summarize and Extract Code features run entirely on your device using Apple's frameworks. Your content is never sent to a server and never leaves your device.

Exports

Exports (PDF, HTML, or Markdown) are created only when you tap export, and go only where you choose to send them — for example a file, a share sheet, or an app you pick. The app does not send exports anywhere on its own.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date. Since the app collects no data, any change would only ever be to clarify wording or reflect new features.
