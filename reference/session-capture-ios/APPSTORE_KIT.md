Your AI conversations are doing real work. Session Capture makes them permanent, portable, and shareable with anyone.

Paste any public AI chat share link from Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini into the built-in browser, tap Capture, and the app extracts the entire conversation. SHARE WITHOUT THE SIGN-IN WALL
Share links expire, get revoked, or force colleagues to log in before they can read anything. Session Capture exports remove that friction entirely. Send a transcript to anyone and they simply open it. No account, no app, no login. Ever.

PDF: pixel-faithful with images embedded, ready to attach, print, or archive.
Self-contained web page: one file that opens in any browser, everything included.
Markdown: clean and ready for your notes app, wiki, docs, or repo.

BUILT FOR CROSS-MODEL RESEARCH

A PERMANENT LOCAL ARCHIVE
Everything you capture lives on your device. Your library is yours: it does not depend on a vendor keeping a share link alive, and it works offline once captured.

OPTIONAL ON-DEVICE INTELLIGENCE
Summarize a long session or extract every code block with optional Apple Intelligence actions. These run only when you tap them and entirely on your device. The app never sends your data anywhere.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts. No tracking. No analytics. No subscriptions and no in-app purchases. Pay once, own it. The only data that ever leaves your device is an export you create and choose to share yourself.

1. Open a public AI chat share link in the built-in browser.

## 5. App Review Notes

Session Capture is a personal capture-and-export utility. The user pastes a PUBLIC AI chat share link (Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini) into the app's built-in WebKit browser, views the page exactly as any browser would, and taps Capture to extract the visible conversation into an on-device library. There is no server-side scraping, no backend, and no account system: the app makes no network requests of its own beyond the WebKit page load the user initiates, and it never stores credentials — public share links require no login to view. All captured content is stored locally on-device; the only data that leaves the device is a PDF, HTML, or Markdown export the user explicitly creates and shares via the system share sheet. Optional Summarize and Extract Code actions use on-device Apple Intelligence only, run solely on user tap, and transmit nothing. WebKit browsing is core functionality, not incidental: rendering the shared conversation page in-app is how the user navigates to and views the content they are capturing, and capture operates on that rendered page. To test: open any public share link from claude.ai/share, chatgpt.com/share, or gemini.google.com/share in the in-app browser and tap Capture. No demo account is needed.
