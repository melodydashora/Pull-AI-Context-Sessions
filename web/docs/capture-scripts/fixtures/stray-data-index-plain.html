What can I put in a compost bin, and what should stay out of it? I have a small garden and a closed plastic bin.

Vegetable scraps, fruit peel, leaves, grass cuttings and coffee grounds all work well. Keep out meat, dairy and cooked food, because they attract pests. Mix green and brown material in roughly equal parts and turn the heap every two weeks. EXAMPLE answer.

How long until it is ready?

In a closed bin, about six months in warm weather and up to a year in a cold one. EXAMPLE answer.
